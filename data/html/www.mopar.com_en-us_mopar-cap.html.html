Developing the technicians of tomorrow

The Mopar® Career Automotive Program (CAP) is partnered with 100 colleges and technical schools nationwide. The Mopar CAP program provides students with more than a general automotive education. By incorporating the Mopar CAP curriculum, schools give their students the ability to turn their passion into a career. Mopar CAP students gain the training and fundamental knowledge they need to work on some of the hottest vehicles in the industry from our legendary Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep®, Ram, FIAT® and Alfa Romeo brands.

Mopar CAP certification gives students a competitive edge and shows our dealers they have the fundamental qualifications to be a successful automotive Technician at an FCA dealership. With educators certified by FCA US LLC, Mopar CAP combines hands-on experience and classroom instruction. Our mission began in 1984, and we continuously strive to train talented students as they prepare for their careers at a Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep, Ram, FIAT and Alfa Romeo dealership.

Mopar® CAP curriculum

The Mopar® CAP curriculum provides a combination of cutting-edge classroom instruction, web-based training and hands-on experience with FCA US LLC vehicles.

In addition to vehicle technology, diagnostics and repairs, you’ll learn other skills and tools needed for a successful career at a Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep®, Ram, FIAT® or Alfa Romeo dealership:

  • Knowledge of automotive systems and components
  • Excellent communication skills
  • Above average mechanical aptitude
  • Tactful problem solving
Program options

Available through participating community colleges, technical colleges or vocational schools nationwide, Mopar® CAP is a certified technical training program.

You’ll receive the latest training from instructors certified in FCA US LLC training. Depending on the individual school’s curriculum and your specific situation, you could complete the program with a certificate or associate degree within 12-24 months. This also means less potential debt upon graduation than that of a four-year degree.
