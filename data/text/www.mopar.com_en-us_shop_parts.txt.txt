FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Whom does Mopar® make parts for?
Mopar® offers genuine parts and accessories designed, engineered, and tested specifically for Chrysler, Jeep®, Dodge, Ram, and FIAT brand vehicles.
What does OEM parts mean?
OEM stands for original equipment manufacturer--and Mopar® is the OEM provider of parts and accessories for Chrysler, Jeep®, Dodge, Ram, and FIAT brand vehicles.
Why use Mopar® parts?
Mopar® parts are designed and engineered by the same teams that design and engineer Chrysler, Jeep®, Dodge, Ram and FIAT brand vehicles. In addition, all parts are subject to rigorous testing to ensure adherence to our OEM standards.
How long are Mopar® parts covered?
For complete details on Mopar® parts warranty coverage by product type, visit https://www.moparrepairconnect.com/mechanical/product-information/warranty/
How to find discontinued Mopar® parts?
We suggest contacting the Parts Department of your authorized Stellantis dealership to explore available inventory or seek additional avenues. If you are searching for restoration or vintage parts, please visit https://www.mopar.com/en-us/shop/parts/mopar-restoration-parts.html
