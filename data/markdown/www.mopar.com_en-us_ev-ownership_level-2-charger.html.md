LEVEL 2 CHARGING UNITS

The Free2move Charge Level 2 charging stations are available in either 40 amp or 48 amp and can deliver a full charge up to six times faster than a Level 1 charging unit. The available charging systems hold a coveted ENERGY STAR® Certification, and are compact, lightweight. The 40A charging station can accommodate either plug-in or hardwired installation. The 48A charges 20% faster than the 40A Level 2 charging station.

40AMP home charging station

Upgrade to a Free2move Charge 40A home charging station which accommodates either plug-in or hardwired installation, and charge your vehicle even faster.

* — UP TO SIX TIMES FASTER CHARGING THAN LEVEL 1 CHARGING.
* — ENERGY STAR® CERTIFIED.
* — EASY TO USE AND INSTALL: Small lightweight, easy to mount unit accommodates either plug-in or hardwired installation and is weatherproof for indoor/outdoor charging.
* — RELIABLE: Assembled in the USA and backed by outstanding customer service.
* — Potentially qualifies for the 30% Federal Tax Credit (up to $1,000) for equipment and installation. May require vehicle purchase; consult your tax professional for more information.
