Helping You Stay Prepared
-------------------------

When an unexpected accident occurs, being armed with the right information can help you navigate the process and help get you and your vehicle safely back on the road.

* [BEFORE AN ACCIDENT](#plugin-tab)
* [AT THE SCENE](#dodge-plugin-tab)
* [AFTER AN ACCIDENT](#plugin-tab2)

Understand Your Insurance Policy
--------------------------------

  

* When an accident happens, the last thing you want is to worry about your vehicle. Check whether your insurance policy covers authentic OEM parts.
* By ensuring your repairs are made using Mopar® collision parts, you'll have the advantage of quality parts engineered specifically for your vehicle.
  
  

Prepare for the Unexpected
--------------------------

  
* It's not always possible to think clearly at the scene of an accident, but always be ready to document an accident with the right tools on hand.
* Keep  [printed accident documentation forms](/content/dam/mopar/pdf/collision-assistance/accidentreportform.pdf)  in your glovebox.

![Dodge Plugin Charger](/content/dam/mopar/images/collision-page-redesign/At-The-Scene-426x342.jpg)

### Take Care of Yourself and Know Who to Contact

  

* Take a moment to make sure you and your passengers are okay. If needed, dial 9-1-1 immediately. Turn on your hazard lights and move to a safe spot if possible.
* Call the police to report the accident. Contact Roadside Assistance at 1-800-521-2779 if you require a tow and be sure to note your preferred dealer/body shop.
  
  

### Document the Necessary Details

  
* Capture the full name and address, license plate number, license/ID and insurance details of the other driver, along with information from any witnesses.
* Take photos and use a  [printed forms](/content/dam/mopar/pdf/collision-assistance/accidentreportform.pdf)   or other means to fully assess your vehicle and take notes of any damage.

![Dodge Plugin Charger](/content/dam/mopar/images/collision-page-redesign/After-An-Accident-426x342.jpg)

Know Your Rights—Request OEM Parts
----------------------------------

  

* You always have the right to request OEM parts to ensure optimum functionality in your vehicle—engineered by the people who know your vehicle best—and to avoid the  [risks of counterfeit parts.](https://www.repairerdrivennews.com/2020/12/01/a2c2-warns-of-counterfeit-auto-parts-in-new-white-paper/)
* Lease terms also require the use of OEM parts, and for added peace of mind, Mopar® offers FlexCare Vehicle Protection Plans.
  
  

Choose Mopar® Certified Repair Facilities
-----------------------------------------

  
* Look for an  [FCA US LLC Certified Shop](/en-us/assistance/collision-assistance.html#find-a-dealer-page)   when your vehicle needs repairs. Remember—you always have the right to choose your own repair facility.
* With our certified body shops, you’ll benefit from highly-trained technicians with the expertise and tools to get you and your vehicle back on the road safely.

Durability And Performance
--------------------------

The quality of an aftermarket part cannot compare to an authentic Mopar® part. We performed a 12-week corrosion test on a Mopar® fender and an aftermarket fender. Both fenders were scratched and exposed to extreme conditions designed to simulate 5-years of exposure to snow, humidity, salt and fog. The results undeniably show the durability of the Mopar® part.

Structural Integrity And Safety
-------------------------------

Fascias that won't warp. Headlamps that properly align and output optimum light for driver safety. High-strength sheet metals that are integral to a durable chassis structure. Mirrors that guide you with a crash avoidance system. Mopar® parts are all built with the highest quality materials and tested to meet safety and structural guidelines that are difficult to reproduce in the aftermarket.

The Perfect Fit
---------------

Whether it's a fender or a headlamp - when a part fits right, it performs better...it's really that simple. Take Mopar® glass, for example. Because the windows are designed specifically for FCA US LLC products, they have the optimum fit in your vehicle, which can prevent a variety of problems in the long run - from leaks and unwanted wind or road noise, to water damage and corrosion. Non-Mopar® parts that deviate from FCA US LLC specifications can require extensive rework for acceptable appearance, fit and finish.
