[The Blog
========](/en-us/blog.html)

When it comes to maintaining the performance, safety, and longevity of your vehicle, choosing the right parts is crucial. That’s why Mopar® stands out as your go-to source for all things automotive. Our commitment to quality and compatibility helps make sure that every replacement and upgrade is the perfect match for your ride.

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/blogs/2-(1).jpg)

Why Choose OEM Parts and Accessories?

Original Equipment Manufacturer (OEM) parts are designed specifically for your vehicle by the manufacturer. Unlike aftermarket parts, OEM parts offer a secure fit and function because they are the exact parts used when your vehicle was assembled. This helps ensure that your vehicle stays healthy—both in the short-term and down the road.

Why Mopar?

Mopar parts are carefully engineered to enhance vehicles without compromising aesthetics, functionality, or performance. These parts meet rigorous testing standards and are made from high-quality materials, helping ensure longevity while providing value. Only Mopar has exclusive access to your vehicle’s specifications, manufacturing parts that accurately fit your vehicle. Plus, we make installation simple by using existing openings and weld nuts, removing the need for additional modifications and maintaining your vehicle’s original structure.

Warranty, Reliability and Testing

Mopar offers a comprehensive [warranty program](https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/warranty/information.html), including a 3-Year/36,000-Mile Basic Limited Warranty[1] and additional coverage for specific parts like batteries and brake pads. These warranties can help protect against material or workmanship defects. In addition to warranty, Mopar’s commitment to reliability is reinforced by rigorous [durability testing](https://blog.mopar.com/what-goes-into-vehicle-durability-testing/), focusing on how the parts and accessories react to real-life scenarios—from the everyday commute to off-roading fun.

Common Replacement Parts

Headlights

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/blogs/3.jpg)

Headlights are a necessity that will eventually need replaced over time. And since every successful route starts with visibility, Mopar headlights are designed to help provide maximum clarity and longevity. Whether you’re navigating through fog or need help illuminating dark country roads, we’ve got your back. We offer a variety of OEM Jeep® brand headlights, Ram 1500 headlights, and much more, all tailored to fit seamlessly onto your ride.

Oil Filters

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/blogs/4-(1).jpg)

Every vehicle needs regular oil changes—even if you’re a DIY kind of person. We hear that, which is why we offer oil filter options for numerous vehicle brands and models. Our oil filters are designed for your specific make and model, providing top-notch filtration to keep your engine running smoothly while helping to extend its lifespan.

Wiper Blades

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/blogs/5-(1).jpg)

It’s easy to forget that wiper blades need some love, too. Whether you’re hitting the trails or staying on pavement, we’ve got OEM wipers to ensure clear visibility. Engineered for durability and next-level performance, our wiper blades can help you maintain a clear view of the road in adverse weather conditions. In addition to wiping away the rain, Jeep® brand wiper blades by Jeep Performance Parts are also built to combat heavy mud and debris that accompanies off-roading adventures.

Choose Mopar for All Your Vehicle Needs

At Mopar, we understand the importance of maintaining the integrity of your vehicle. That’s why we offer a wide range of parts and accessories that meet the strictest standards of quality and performance. Trust Mopar for all your vehicle upgrades and replacements—because no one knows these vehicles better than the OEM.

Visit our [eStore](https://store.mopar.com/) or contact your local dealer to explore our full range of products.
