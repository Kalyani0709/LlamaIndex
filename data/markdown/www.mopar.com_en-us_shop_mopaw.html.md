Introducing Mopaw™
------------------

Mopaw™ is an exciting addition to the Mopar® family, offering a new line of premium-quality travel- and vehicle-related pet accessories. All products, whether exclusively designed for Mopaw™ or sourced from prominent manufacturers, meet Mopar’s high standards for quality, durability and vehicle compatibility, creating a unique feeling of buyer’s confidence.

[Browse offerings](#featured-products)
[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/mopaw-pet-supplies)

![image alt](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/carrier.jpg)

PET CARRIER

Both you and your pet can chill with our pet carrier sourced from Nemesys Gear. Made to be compatible with your vehicle’s back seat latch system, it allows you to safely transport your furry friends and help leave anxiety behind.

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/affiliated-accessories-pet-carrier-sold-by-nemesys-gear-68740960aa?c=bD0xJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)

![image alt](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/partition.jpg)

PET PARTITION

Give your pets their own space and keep distractions at bay with our Mopaw™-exclusive pet partition. Easily installed or removed, this durable nylon net acts as a barrier when fastened to tie-down loops, turning your vehicle’s cargo area into Fido’s favorite lounge.

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/mopar-pet-partition-82215378ab?c=bD0xJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/kennel.jpg)

PET KENNEL

Pets and drivers agree: traveling feels more secure in an enclosed space. With four tie-down straps that secure it to your vehicle, this unique, Mopaw™-exclusive folding pet kennel conveniently stores flat and is quickly assembled for your next four-legged adventure.

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/mopar-pet-kennel-82213729ac?c=bD0yJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/ramp.jpg)

PET RAMP

Tails will wag, backs will be saved. The WeatherTech® Pet Ramp was designed for the comfort of our furry friends and their handlers. The foldable, portable Pet Ramp features a non-slip surface providing dogs, cats and pets of all sizes easy access to SUVs, minivans, trucks and hard to reach household spaces.

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/affiliated-accessories-weathertech-pet-ramp-sold-by-competition-specialties-68752455aa?c=bD0xJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/barrier.jpg)

PET BARRIER

Your vehicle wasn’t meant to be a dog park: help keep it protected and your pet calm with the WeatherTech® Pet Barrier. Adjustable and expandable, this heavy-duty barrier keeps pets safely secured in the rear of your vehicle with no tools or drilling needed.

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/affiliated-accessories-weathertech-pet-barrier-sold-by-competition-specialties-68752441aa?c=bD0xJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)

![](/content/dam/mopar/images/mopaw/feeding.jpg)

PET BOWLS

Treat your pets to elevated dining with the Double High Pet Feeding System from WeatherTech®. A specially designed bowl stand raises pet bowls to an optimal height, giving pets a more comfortable eating and drinking experience. The unique shape of these bowls prevents neck strain while cats and dogs eat or drink, and a protective mat features a raised outer lip to catch any spills. Designed, engineered and manufactured in America using NSF-approved and human food grade materials, including two stainless steel or BPA-Free plastic bowls. Bone appétit!

[Shop Now](https://store.mopar.com/oem-parts/affiliated-accessories-weathertech-pet-feeding-system-double-high-dark-grey-32oz-sold-by-competition-specialties-68752444aa?c=bD0xJm49U2VhcmNoIFJlc3VsdHM%3D)
