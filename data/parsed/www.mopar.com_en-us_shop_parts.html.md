# Whom does Mopar® make parts for?

Mopar® offers genuine parts and accessories designed, engineered, and tested specifically for Chrysler, Jeep®, Dodge, Ram, and FIAT brand vehicles.

# What does OEM parts mean?

OEM stands for original equipment manufacturer--and Mopar® is the OEM provider of parts and accessories for Chrysler, Jeep®, Dodge, Ram, and FIAT brand vehicles.
