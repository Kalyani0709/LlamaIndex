# Uconnect® Bluetooth Pairing Guide

1. Complete the following steps on the system:
1. First, press the 'Phone' icon on the Uconnect® Touch Screen.
2. Press 'Yes'
2. Complete the following steps in the phone:
1. Select 'Uconnect' as the paired device.

3. Complete the following steps on the system:
1. Once paired, you can set your phone as the favorite by pressing 'Yes.'
2. Your phone is now paired and ready for hands-free calling.
3. Press the Uconnect Phone button on your steering wheel to begin.

# Helpful tips to improve Bluetooth performance with your Uconnect® system

# Your phone won't pair to system

- Perform a hard reset in the phone by removing the battery (if removable – see your phone's owner's manual).
- Delete the pairing history in your phone and Uconnect® system, which is usually found in the phone's Bluetooth® settings.
- Verify you are selecting “Uconnect” in the discovered Bluetooth devices on your phone.

# Your phone won't reconnect to the system after pairing

- Set your phone to "auto-connect" or "trusted device" within the phone's Bluetooth® settings.
- Perform a factory reset on your phone. Refer to your phone manufacturer or cellular provider for instructions.
- Many phones don't automatically reconnect after being restarted. Your phone can still be connected manually.

# Your phonebook didn't download

- Check “Do not ask again," then accept the “phonebook download” request on your phone.
- Up to 2000 contact names with four numbers per contact will transfer to the system's phonebook. Check with your phone manufacturer or cellular provider to avoid having duplicate contacts stored on your phone.

Text messaging through your system doesn't work




● Check “Do not ask again," then accept the “connect to your messages” request on your phone.

# You can't make a conference call*

● Please keep in mind, Verizon, Sprint and US Cellular do not support conference calling.

# You can't make a call while connected to the AUX jack

● Plugging in your phone to AUX while connected to Bluetooth® will disable Hands-Free Calling. Do not make calls while your phone is plugged into the AUX jack.

# You're having trouble streaming music via Bluetooth®

● Before driving, start the music app on your phone. Your phone may have closed the app to conserve battery. Restart it by opening the music app and pressing play.

# Disconnecting a Smartphone or an Audio Device

1. Press the Pairing button on the touchscreen.
2. Press the Paired Phones and Audio Devices buttons on the touchscreen.
3. Press the Settings button located to the right of the device name.
4. The options pop-up will be displayed.
5. Press the Disconnect Phone button on the touchscreen.
6. Press the X to exit out of the Settings screen.

# Deleting a Phone or an Audio Device

1. Press the Pairing button on the touchscreen.
2. Press the Paired Phones and Audio Devices button on the touchscreen.
3. Press the Settings button located to the right of the device name for a different Phone or Audio Device than the currently connected device.
4. The options pop-up will be displayed.
5. Press the Delete Phone button on the touchscreen.
6. Press the X to exit out of the Settings screen.

# Disconnecting a Specific Smartphone or Bluetooth® Audio Device

1. Access the Settings menu.
2. Select the Phone/Bluetooth button.
3. Select the Paired Phones or Paired Audio list using the corresponding button on the display.
4. Select the specific device (smartphone or Bluetooth device).
5. Press the Disconnect button.

# Deletion of a Mobile Phone or a Bluetooth® Audio Device

1. Access the Settings menu.
2. Select the Phone/Bluetooth button.
3. Select the Paired Phones or Paired Audio list using the corresponding button on the display.
4. Select the device (mobile phone or Bluetooth device).
5. Press the Delete Device button.
6. A confirmation screen will appear on the display:
- Press the Yes button to delete the device.
- Press the No button to cancel the operation.

# How do I reset the smartphone pairing connection on my vehicle?

Complete the following steps to reset the Bluetooth connection:

1. Open the Bluetooth menu on the vehicle system.
2. Select 'Forget' or 'Delete' the phone(s) from the list of Bluetooth devices stored in the vehicle system.
3. Pair the car and your device again.

For detailed help use the Mopar Bluetooth site https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/bluetooth-pairing.html to look up your phone. You will find exact instructions tailored for your phone model and vehicle system.

# Why won't my phone stay connected to my vehicle?

If you are experiencing connection issues or connections are dropped after connection, make sure the devices are as close as possible. Phones will connect more reliably when close to the in-car system. You should also ensure there are no obstructions between the phone and car, and pairing issues can also occur if the phone is in a pocket or bag. Proximity is particularly important when it comes to pairing.

# Why does my Android Auto keep disconnecting from the vehicle?

This is a known issue with some specific phone models, please check for an updated Android version for your phone. Manually reconnect the phone in Bluetooth settings and select the car system from the phone's Bluetooth paired device list.

# Why won't my smartphone connect to my vehicle?

Make sure the phone you want to use is compatible. Use the Mopar site https://www.mopar.com/en-us/care/bluetooth-pairing.html to look up your phone and check its performance. Very occasionally a phone will not be compatible with your car system. More frequently you may find certain functions are not supported. The list of supported functions is displayed for most popular phones in the Bluetooth connectivity center. After finding a phone you can view a detailed list of supported functions and view a help guide.
