# We've Got You Covered

The extensive Dodge warranty* coverage plans make it so that you can go farther and harder with your Dodge vehicle. Keep it up. There is no boring in going.

# 5-YEAR / 60,000-MILE POWERTRAIN LIMITED WARRANTY

We back every Dodge Brand vehicle with the 5-Year/60,000-Mile Powertrain Limited Warranty*. It covers the cost of all parts and labor needed to repair a covered powertrain component. Coverage also includes towing to the nearest Chrysler, Dodge, Jeep® or Ram vehicle dealer, if necessary. The warranty is transferable too, so if you sell your vehicle during the warranty period, the new owner will be covered. For details, see your Dodge dealer.

# POWERTRAIN LIMITED WARRANTY COVERAGE

# Gas Engines

The cylinder block and all internal parts of gasoline engines, including the intake and exhaust manifolds, the oil and water pump and the supercharger, are covered under your Powertrain Limited Warranty*.

# Transmission

The transmission case and all internal parts, including the torque converter, pressure and speed sensors and the transmission range switch, are covered under your Powertrain Limited Warranty.

# Drive Systems

The different drive systems and internal parts, including front-wheel drive, all-wheel drive, rear-wheel drive and four-wheel drive, are covered under your Powertrain Limited Warranty.

# 3-YEAR/36,000-MILE BASIC LIMITED WARRANTY

The 3-Year/36,000-Mile Basic Limited Warranty* provides bumper-to-bumper coverage for your vehicle, from the body to the electrical system. The warranty is also transferable, and it includes towing, if necessary.

# ROADSIDE ASSISTANCE SERVICE

Most 2016 Dodge Brand vehicles come with the 5-Year/100,000-Mile Roadside Assistance Service**. 2017 models offer Roadside Assistance Service for five years or 60,000 miles, whichever comes first. When you need support with a battery jumpstart, roadside fuel delivery, flat tire service or towing to the nearest FCA US LLC vehicle dealer, help is only a phone call away. For details, see your Dodge dealer.
